## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes academic prestige, civic heritage, and high-performance enterprise SaaS engineering. Derived directly from the institutional crest—anchored by the motto *"Endeavor to Excel"*—the aesthetic merges deep institutional dignity with the crispness and density of modern operational software.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Built upon deep heraldic navy and warm gold accents that evoke decades of educational integrity, institutional reliability, and serious administrative governance.
- **Precision-Driven & Productive:** Balances dense, high-frequency school operations (admissions, fee schedules, CBSE grading, timetable scheduling, daily attendance) with breathable clarity and effortless ergonomics.
- **Warm & Scholarly:** Human-centered, approachable for faculty and parents alike, utilizing clean warm slate surfaces and accessible status semantics over cold technical minimalism.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design movement is **Modern Institutional SaaS**: structured structural layouts, crisp low-contrast slate dividers, multi-tiered subtle ambient shadows, and purposeful micro-accents. Interfaces prioritize data legibility, fast scanability across data tables, prominent KPI summaries, and frictionless multi-role workflows across administrators, teachers, accountants, and guardians.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the heraldic crest while integrating high-functioning SaaS color tokens engineered for high accessibility (WCAG AA/AAA compliance across all text contrasts).

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Royal Navy (`#0A1931` / surface `#152238`):** Anchors headers, primary command buttons, side navigation rails, and core structural brand elements.
- **Secondary — Regal Mustard Gold (`#F5A623` / deep `#D97706`):** Reserved for high-value highlights, active focus borders, critical fee notices, scholarship indicators, and standout primary actions.
- **Tertiary — Emerald Green (`#10B981` / background `#ECFDF5`):** Denotes present attendance marks, successful fee clearances, approved leaves, and positive performance metrics.
- **Neutral Palette:** Neutral slate spans from pure `#FFFFFF` through canvas `#F8FAFC`, subtle stroke `#E2E8F0`, muted text `#64748B`, to deep text `#0F172A`.

### Vibrant Functional & Analytical Accents
- **Soft Crimson (`#EF4444` / surface `#FEF2F2`):** Absences, overdue fee collections, disciplinary alerts, system errors.
- **Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5` / surface `#EEF2FF`):** Examination schedules, syllabus modules, academic curricula.
- **Vibrant Teal (`#0D9488` / surface `#F0FDFA`):** Library management, inventory, co-curricular sports.
- **Amethyst Violet (`#8B5CF6` / surface `#F5F3FF`):** Executive reporting, analytics, trend distribution charts.
- **Sky Blue (`#0284C7` / surface `#F0F9FF`):** Bus tracking, GPS transport, communication broadcasts.

## Typography

Typography establishes an unwavering institutional hierarchy while sustaining effortless scannability across high-density administrative panels.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric balance, friendly yet authoritative stroke width, and crisp rendering on digital screens. It brings contemporary prestige to page titles, dashboard metrics, student report card headers, and module headers.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Engineered for ultra-high legibility in complex data environments. Tabular numbers (`tnum`), crisp x-heights, and open apertures ensure that student roll numbers, marks, currency sums (₹ INR), and timetable slots remain instantly distinct.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase label tokens (`label-sm`, `label-md` when used in table headings or pill tags) apply `+0.04em` tracking to prevent optical clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **Fluid 12-Column Responsive Grid** anchored around efficient administrative usage patterns.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Framing
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout; gutter `1rem`; page margin `1rem`. Collapsible sidebars shift into bottom navigation bars or full-height off-canvas drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout; gutter `1.25rem`; page margin `1.5rem`. Secondary panels collapse to toggleable sheets.
- **Desktop (1024px - 1440px+):** 12-column system; gutter `1.5rem`; outer canvas margin `2rem`. Supports a persistent 260px Royal Navy navigation rail, optional contextual right drawer (e.g., student quick dossier), and flexible main data canvas.

### Spacing Rhythm
Component spacing follows strict 4px/8px incremental multiples:
- Micro margins (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) govern input internals, table row padding, and badge clusters.
- Container padding (`space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px) controls card interiors, modal sheets, and KPI metric widgets.
- Macro spacing (`space-xl` = 40px) demarcates distinct operational sections on the canvas.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via a **Tonal Layer + Ambient Multi-Tier Shadow** system that gives cards physical presence without adding visual noise.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` background. Elements rely on `#E2E8F0` hairline strokes (1px) with zero shadow for base segregation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Quick Dossier Cards):** Raised cards, hovered rows, and popovers: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Select Menus & Dropdowns):** Floating options: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Emergency Alerts):** Centered dialogues with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px); background: rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.4)`): shadow `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.06)`.

Shadows are tinted with the deep Primary Navy (`rgba(10, 25, 49, ...)`) instead of neutral grey to maintain institutional cohesion.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature standard, communicating contemporary polish while preserving clean geometric density.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (8px / `0.5rem`):** Inputs, select menus, dropdown items, standard action buttons, table cell badge holders, and tooltip shells.
- **Cards & Data Panels (12px / `0.75rem`):** All KPI widgets, student cards, timetable containers, attendance grids, and modal dialogs.
- **Status Badges & Avatar Containers (`9999px` / Full Pill):** Attendance tags (`Present`, `Absent`, `Late`), role markers (`Staff`, `Admin`, `Parent`), and round profile portraits.
- **Focus Rings:** 2px solid Gold (`#F5A623`) offset by 2px white space for high-visibility keyboard accessibility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Royal Navy background (`#0A1931`), white text, 8px radius, height 40px (desktop) / 44px (touch). Hover shifts to `#152238` with shadow Level 2.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Regal Gold (`#F5A623`), navy text (`#0A1931`), font weight 600. Reserved for high-intent actions (e.g., "Collect Fee", "Publish Exam Results").
- **Outline / Neutral:** Transparent background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, slate text (`#334155`). Hover introduces `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Soft Crimson (`#EF4444`) with white text, or white background with Crimson border and text for secondary destructive tasks.

### Metric KPI Cards
- Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`), 12px corner radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, Level 1 shadow.
- Upper row: Metric title (`label-md`, `#64748B`) paired with a colored pastel-tinted icon container (36x36px, e.g., `#ECFDF5` for Emerald Green or `#FEF2F2` for Crimson).
- Core value: Large bold numeral (`headline-lg`, `#0F172A`).
- Bottom row: Micro trend indicator with up/down arrows (e.g., `+4.2% from last term` in Emerald Green or Sky Blue).

### Data Tables
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-sm`, tracking `0.05em`, color `#64748B`, height 44px.
- **Rows:** Zebra-less white surface, 1px border-bottom `#E2E8F0`, height 52px for optimal clickability. Hover state transitions row background to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Numeric Alignment:** Right-aligned with tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Inline Actions:** Right-aligned icon buttons inside transparent circles with hover states.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding 2px 10px, typography `label-sm`.
- **Present / Passed:** Emerald Green text (`#065F46`) on pale emerald background (`#D1FAE5`).
- **Absent / Overdue:** Soft Crimson text (`#991B1B`) on light red background (`#FEE2E2`).
- **Late / Pending:** Amber Gold text (`#92400E`) on warm amber background (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Subject / Cohort Tags:** Electric Indigo or Sky Blue muted tints.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- 40px height, 8px radius, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `body-md` (`#0F172A`).
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Border color changes to `#0A1931` with an outer focus-ring in Regal Gold (`#F5A623`).
- Error state: Border color changes to `#EF4444` with helper text rendered in `body-sm` crimson.

### Timetable & Attendance Grids
- Interactive schedule grid featuring 12px rounded cards inside dynamic weekday columns.
- Current period highlighted with a 3px left border accent in Regal Amber (`#F5A623`).
- Quick-toggle attendance switchers utilizing standard 24px circular buttons: Green "P", Red "A", and Yellow "L".
