Why Permissions Are Rigidly Bounded by Role (Rule SEC-402)

Unlike permissive commercial software that permits arbitrary permission checkboxes per user, HanuJai High School's ERP strictly enforces Hierarchical Functional Boundaries. This structural safeguard guarantees that:

visibility_off Zero Peer Marks Intrusion

Subject teachers cannot inspect marks entered by colleagues. This eliminates unintentional bias and internal student grading disputes.

credit_card_off Total Bursary Insulation

Instructional faculty have absolute zero exposure to fee balances or defaulter registries, protecting students from classroom discrimination.

history_edu Gazette Tamper-Proofing

Marks cannot be altered once the examination window elapses without dual-approval cryptographic commit logged directly to institutional server.

shield_person Strict Cohort Isolation

A Class Teacher’s pastoral oversight is physically restricted to exactly 1 sanctioned classroom section per academic year.
