## Brand & Style

This design system translates prestigious institutional heritage into a premier native Android Academic ERP experience. Designed specifically for Android phones and foldables using Material 3 ergonomic standards, it brings administrative rigor, academic trust, and operational clarity to teachers, principals, and administrative staff on the move.

### Target Audience & Core Experience
- **Audience:** School administrators, teachers, coordinators, accountants, and guardians managing daily classroom rosters, grading rubrics, attendance tracking, and fee authorizations.
- **Emotional Response:** Authoritative confidence, calm operational control, zero friction during rapid field entry (e.g., morning roll call in under 90 seconds).
- **Design Philosophy:** **Modern Institutional Material**—combining Material 3 dynamic ergonomics, high-contrast heraldic accents, crisp 48dp tactile targets, structured tonal surfaces, and pill status chips that preserve dignity while delivering enterprise density.

## Colors

The color palette adapts the school's heritage crest into high-performance, WCAG AAA compliant chromatic tokens designed for bright classroom lighting and quick OLED viewing.

### Palette Strategy
- **Primary — Royal Navy (`#0A1931`):** Grounds top app bars, primary bottom navigation active states, high-priority Floating Action Buttons (FAB), and modal sheet headers.
- **Secondary — Regal Amber Gold (`#F5A623`):** Reserved for high-value highlights, primary submission CTA buttons, pending approvals, and active focus boundaries.
- **Tertiary — Emerald Green (`#10B981`):** Communicates present attendance, cleared financial dues, passed grades, and sync confirmation statuses.
- **Neutral Foundations (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** Form crisp warm slate surfaces avoiding harsh pure blacks, keeping visual fatigue low during long grading sessions.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - **Soft Crimson (`#EF4444` / `#FEF2F2`):** Flagged absentees, overdue fee alerts, error feedback.
  - **Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5` / `#EEF2FF`):** Examination milestones, syllabus trackers, teacher notes.
  - **Sky Blue (`#0284C7` / `#F0F9FF`):** Transport, fleet tracking, and real-time broadcast indicators.

## Typography

Typography pairs institutional presence with strict mobile operational data legibility.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines):** Supplies geometric character and modern prestige to screen headers, KPI numeric rollouts, and modal sheet titles.
- **Inter (Body & Labels):** Delivers clean readability for high-density academic ledgers, roll numbers, CBSE grading scales, and parent contact logs. Body numbers and currency figures strictly leverage `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure marks and percentages align vertically down continuous mobile lists.
- **Micro-Labels:** `label-sm` utilizes an uppercase transformation paired with `+0.04em` tracking for badge tags and filter chips.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to native Android Material 3 mobile ergonomics with an emphasis on one-handed thumb-zone reachability.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile Handsets (< 600dp):** Fluid 4-column layout, 16dp margins, 16dp gutters. Interactive primary triggers (save, submit, filter, next) reside within bottom app bars or sticky bottom sheets.
- **Tablets & Large Foldables (600dp - 1024dp):** Fluid 8-column layout, 24dp margins, 20dp gutters. Adapts to split master-detail views (e.g., student roster on the left, individual marks breakdown on the right).

### Tap Targets & Ergonomic Rules
- All interactive controls (buttons, attendance toggles, icon triggers, chip filters) enforce a strict minimum touch target of **48dp × 48dp** to eliminate mis-taps during rapid classroom walk-throughs.
- Vertical lists use consistent 8dp and 12dp row gaps to optimize swipe velocity without visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built on tonal layering combined with soft, Primary-Navy tinted ambient shadows rather than harsh neutral drop-shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (App Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Unraised base canvas.
- **Level 1 (Resting Card / List Tile):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border and ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Pressed Card, Bottom App Bar):** `#FFFFFF` surface, elevated with: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Button & Sticky Quick Dossiers):** Deep Navy or Gold FAB: `0 8px 16px -2px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.06)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Full Dialogs):** Backdrop scrim `rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.5)` with 4px Gaussian blur; sheet container: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 25, 49, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape strategy to blend modern Android Material 3 geometry with institutional structure.

### Corner Radius System
- **Compact Elements (8dp / `rounded-DEFAULT`):** Text fields, search inputs, dropdown menus, and standard square buttons.
- **Containers & Cards (12dp to 16dp / `rounded-lg`):** Student roster cards, metric tiles, grade summary containers, and bottom sheet top edges (24dp).
- **Pill Badges & Circular Indicators (9999px / `rounded-full`):** Attendance badges (`P`, `A`, `L`), filter chips, circular roll avatars, and Floating Action Buttons.

## Components

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Native 80dp Android M3 bottom navigation docked to the screen bottom.
- 4 primary destinations: **Home/Dashboard**, **Roster/Attendance**, **Academics/Grades**, and **Admin/Fees**.
- Active destination indicator: Pill-shaped Royal Navy background (`#0A1931`) with Amber Gold icon tint and high-emphasis label (`label-sm`).
- Inactive items rendered in `#64748B`.

### Attendance Quick-Toggle Roster Item
- White surface card, 12dp radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, 48dp minimum vertical height per action.
- Left edge: Circular 36dp avatar with student initials in Navy bold against soft grey (`#E2E8F0`).
- Center: Student Name (`headline-sm`), roll number (`label-md` in `#64748B`), and house badge.
- Right: Dual segment pill toggle for **Present** (`#10B981` filled icon / emerald text) vs. **Absent** (`#EF4444` filled icon / red text). Tapping provides immediate haptic feedback and real-time counter updates.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary CTA (Submit / Approval):** Full-width or auto-flex 48dp height button. Regal Amber Gold (`#F5A623`) background, Royal Navy bold text (`#0A1931`), 12dp radius.
- **Executive Navy Action:** Royal Navy (`#0A1931`) background with `#FFFFFF` text and Level 2 elevation.
- **Outlined Action:** Transparent background, 1.5dp border `#E2E8F0`, `#0A1931` text with subtle hover/ripple.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Status badges use full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) with 4dp vertical and 10dp horizontal padding.
- **Present / Distinction:** Pale emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald text (`#065F46`).
- **Absent / Critical Due:** Pale crimson background (`#FEF2F2`), bold crimson text (`#991B1B`).
- **Late / In-Progress:** Pale amber background (`#FEF3C7`), bold amber text (`#92400E`).
- **Standard Filter Chip:** Outlined 1px `#E2E8F0` with `#0F172A` text; shifts to `#0A1931` background with white text when selected.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- 48dp height with 12dp radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px `#E2E8F0`.
- Integrated leading search icon (`#64748B`) and clear trailing icon button.
- Focused state: Border transitions to `#0A1931` accompanied by a 2dp Amber Gold (`#F5A623`) outer focus glow.

### Metric KPI Carousel
- Horizontally scrollable 140dp width cards for quick mobile scanning.
- Upper label (`label-md`, `#64748B`), bold numeric value (`headline-lg`, `#0F172A`), and delta badge (`+3.8%` in `#10B981`).

### Modal Bottom Sheet
- Anchored to the viewport bottom with a 24dp top-corner radius.
- Includes a centered 32dp × 4dp rounded grab bar (`#CBD5E1`) at the top edge.
- Houses contextual secondary workflows (e.g., student absence remarks, SMS dispatch to guardians, grade rubric detail).
